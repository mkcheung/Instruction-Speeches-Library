Instruction Speeches UX Proposals

Eight changes that would make the coaching loop easier to use. Each one shows the screen as the code renders it today next to the proposed design, followed by the trade-offs.

Reviewed 31 October 2026 on branch development. Labels in the "Current" mockups are copied from the code, and every problem cites the file that causes it. The full findings, including readiness, performance and Docker images, are in PLATFORM-READINESS-REVIEW.md at the repo root.

1

A home that says what to do next

New usersSpeakersEffort S (A) or M (B)

Finishing onboarding sends everyone to /dashboard (Onboarding.tsx:69-72), which is the reviewer's inbox. A new speaker sees four empty sections and no way to upload or invite from there (Dashboard.tsx:96-129). The brand link goes to the same page, and My speeches' empty state is a single line.

Pros

  • The first screen always offers one primary action.
  • The card teaches the upload, invite, watch loop without a tutorial.
  • B gives speakers a place where new feedback shows up.
  • Empty sections stop dominating the page.

Cons and risks

  • Steps 2 and 3, and lane B, need per-speech review counts that SpeechResource doesn't send yet.
  • Renaming "My reviews" touches about 41 references, including 4 end-to-end specs.
  • The copy must never suggest an open pool of reviewers.

Changes: Dashboard.tsx, MySpeeches.tsx, SpeechController@index and SpeechResource (withCount); B also changes lib/roles.ts.

Product rules: no effect on how commentary plays or who sees it.

2

Upload and invite in one flow

SpeakersEffort S to M

The upload ends with "Uploaded. Processing now" and a Go to my speeches button (SpeechCreate.tsx:74,80-82). The card has no link while the video processes, even though inviting doesn't need a ready video (SpeechPolicy.php:22-25). Once ready, the speech opens on Notes, whose empty state says "No reviewers available." Inviting is the fourth tab. Getting to the first invitation takes about six screens and nine clicks.

Pros

  • The first invitation takes one screen instead of about six.
  • The transcode wait gets used instead of wasted.
  • The two circular empty states go away. They also gain an Invite reviewers button and a ?tab=reviewers deep link that notifications can reuse.
  • Cards get an Open button while processing.

Cons and risks

  • Inviting several people means several POST /speeches/{id}/reviews calls or a new batch endpoint.
  • InviteReviewerPanel is single-select today and needs reworking.
  • The unused preview checkbox should be removed or actually built.
  • speech-create.spec.ts covers this route and needs updating.

Changes: SpeechCreate.tsx, InviteReviewerPanel.tsx, SpeechWatch.tsx (read the tab from the URL), MySpeeches.tsx, TrackSelector.tsx, OwnerVideoTools.tsx.

Product rules: every review still starts with a named invitation; there is no open pool.

3

Faster note capture with visible shortcuts

ReviewersCoachesEffort S (A) or M (B)

Every text note takes five pointer trips: Use current time for Start, play, Use current time for End, type, then Add annotation (AnnotationEditor.tsx:43-68). A keyboard map exists (useKeyboardShortcuts.ts), but C only stamps Start, nothing ends or submits, and no screen mentions any shortcut. The form sits below the heading, the timeline and the tool tabs, so on a phone the video is off-screen at the moment of capture.

Pros

  • Roughly half the pointer trips per note.
  • Shortcuts become discoverable on the buttons they replace.
  • On a phone, the video stays in view while capturing.

Cons and risks

  • PLAN-ANNOTATION-RANGE-AUTHORING.md:63 says End must never be filled implicitly, so the +N s buttons must stay deliberate presses.
  • New keys must respect the typing guard in useKeyboardShortcuts.ts:187-207.
  • The accessible name "Use current time for start" is pinned by annotation-range/authoring.spec.ts.
  • B adds a sticky element, which the layout has avoided so far.

Changes: AnnotationEditor.tsx, useAnnotationEditor.ts, useKeyboardShortcuts.ts, new ShortcutSheet.tsx.

Product rules: no effect.

4

One "Review & send" step, with guarded exits

CoachesSpeakersEffort MIncludes a missing notification

Feedback goes out through two buttons in two tabs: Publish feedback under Notes and the essay's own Publish. The essay one notifies nobody: EssayService.php:65-81 only stamps essay_published_at, while notes publishing sends ReviewPublished. Both are irreversible and unconfirmed. Withdraw and Abandon also fire on one click, and nothing explains that one becomes "declined" and the other "abandoned".

Pros

  • One clear moment of sending instead of two easy-to-miss buttons.
  • The essay's arrival finally reaches the speaker.
  • Irreversible actions get a confirmation step.
  • The exits are explained in terms the coach understands.

Cons and risks

  • The backend must notify on the first essay publish, reusing the $wasFirstPublish && $delta > 0 debounce (IMPLEMENTATION-PLANS.md:190).
  • Coaches who publish in batches pay one extra click each time.
  • End-to-end specs reference the "Publish feedback" and "Abandon" labels.

Changes: AnnotationComposerPanel.tsx, EssayEditorPanel.tsx, new SendFeedbackDialog.tsx, Dashboard.tsx, EssayService.php, ReviewPublished.php.

Product rules: no effect; reviewers still see only their own work.

5

One "Feedback from" bar at the video

SpeakersEffort M

The speaker chooses whose commentary plays in Notes › Commentary track (TrackSelector.tsx:30-48), two panels below the video. A second tab bar above it can only say "Choose a reviewer in Notes". The playback-mode pills look exactly like the reviewer pills. The Essay tab repeats the reviewer picker, and the poster picker, a one-time job, holds a tab. In one column, the video scrolls away while the speaker reads notes.

Pros

  • The switch sits next to the video it changes.
  • One picker drives timeline, notes and essay, so the essay's duplicate picker goes away.
  • Two tab bars become one, and an essay dot shows when an essay has arrived.

Cons and risks

  • It must stay a single-select radiogroup, so only one reviewer's commentary ever plays.
  • The accessible names "Choose commentary track" and "Choose whose essay to read" are pinned in end-to-end specs.
  • Fullscreen "Commentary controls" must keep a single owner.
  • B needs fullscreen and iOS testing.

Changes: SpeechWatch.tsx, TrackSelector.tsx, CommentaryPlaybackControls.tsx, OwnerVideoTools.tsx, remove EssayReviewerPicker.tsx, ReviewRosterResource.php (add essay_published_at).

Product rules: keeps "exactly one reviewer's commentary plays at a time" by design: the picker is single-choice.

6

Access and removal in plain language

SpeakersEffort S to MFixes a privacy leak

Roster badges show raw status codes such as in_progress (ReviewerRoster.tsx:149). Each active row has two red buttons. Cancelling an invitation nobody has answered still requires typing a reason. The purge dialog says "This deletes 7 notes, 3 of them published", built from the draft-inclusive annotations_count (RevokeReviewDialog.tsx:104). That tells the speaker how many unpublished drafts the coach has, which the project's own rule forbids. The same leak was already fixed once, in the note-count badge.

Pros

  • Closes the draft-count leak, which is a rule fix and not just polish.
  • The verbs match the situation: cancel an invitation, remove access.
  • One destructive control per row, behind a menu and a typed confirmation.
  • Access becomes visible from My speeches.

Cons and risks

  • The card line needs the same per-speech counts as proposal 1.
  • Canned reasons are sent verbatim, so their wording needs care.
  • The test ids revoke-dialog and revoke-and-purge-dialog must keep working.
  • The roster API itself returns annotations_count to the speaker; the full fix also drops it from ReviewRosterResource.

Changes: ReviewerRoster.tsx, RevokeReviewDialog.tsx, MySpeeches.tsx, SpeechResource.php, ReviewRosterResource.php.

Product rules: restores "the speaker never sees a coach's drafts" (IMPLEMENTATION-PLANS.md:232).

7

A directory you can choose from

SpeakersCoaches being foundEffort S to M

Directory rows show only a name, a username and a role badge (ReviewerDirectory.tsx:92-107), and the invite picker looks the same. The API already sends avatar_url, but no face is drawn. There is no profile link, bio or invite action, so the page is a dead end. Re-inviting someone already invited changes nothing (ReviewService.php:137-139) but still reports "Invitation sent".

Pros

  • Choosing a reviewer becomes a real decision, with face and bio.
  • The social layer finally feeds the core loop.
  • The directory stops being a dead end.
  • No more false "sent" messages.

Cons and risks

  • Bio needs a new resource field, and bio text in a list is a moderation surface (MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md:2033).
  • "Connected" must never reveal that someone blocked you.
  • The role guard must stay first (User.php:101), and the directory must never list anyone's speeches.

Changes: ReviewerDirectory.tsx, InviteReviewerPanel.tsx, ReviewerDirectoryResource.php, ReviewerDirectoryController.php.

Product rules: invitations stay named and explicit; the "Invite to…" menu lists only the speaker's own speeches.

8

Admin decision panel for coach applications

AdminsEffort S

The statement and the documents are separate tabs, and the page opens on Request, even though the documents decide whether Approve is allowed (ViewCoachApplication.php:133-192). Why Approve is disabled is explained only by a hover tooltip, which keyboard and touch users can't reach. Nothing refreshes, so "Scanning" never turns "Clean" without a reload. There is no "next application" and the list doesn't show whether documents are ready.

Pros

  • Evidence and decision are visible together.
  • The blocked state is explained accessibly instead of in a tooltip.
  • Auto-refresh ends the reload guessing.
  • Faster passes through the queue.

Cons and risks

  • It moves away from Filament's modal pattern, so keep a confirm step and Gate::authorize('role.assign').
  • Polling costs queries, so stop once nothing is scanning.
  • Keep the sign-and-audit at click time, and don't preview PDFs inline.

Changes: ViewCoachApplication.php, CoachApplicationResource.php, admin-workspace.css.

Product rules: only an admin can make a coach, after reviewing the uploaded certificates; unchanged.
